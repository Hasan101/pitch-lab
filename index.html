Example below: the synthetic phrase.

Pitch detection: pYIN (probabilistic YIN) with HMM tracking on raw microphone audio (echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain all off), about 60 readings a second. Low notes use a ~100 ms window plus the loudness envelope, so steady vocal fry draws a continuous line down to C1. On synthetic test voices it stays within 1 cent from C1 to C7 and 5 cents up to C8. Many phone mics barely pick up notes below about E1, and very irregular fry can still break up. Use headphones or a quiet room; the mic is muted while reference notes play.
