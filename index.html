Example below: the synthetic phrase.

Pitch detection: two-pass YIN on raw microphone audio (echo cancellation and noise suppression off), median-smoothed, about 60 readings a second. Covers C1 to C8; on synthetic test voices it stays within 1 cent from C1 to C5 and within 6 cents up to C8. Real-world limits: many phone mics barely pick up notes below about E1, and above C6 only whistle register reaches. Use headphones or a quiet room; the mic is muted while reference notes play.
